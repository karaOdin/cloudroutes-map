- Lines & stops is now a drawer rather than a modal. It stays open while stops are tapped, the map behind it is neither dimmed nor blocked, and it is capped shorter so there is map left to look at.
- A tapped stop flies into the middle of the strip of map still visible above the drawer rather than to the centre of a map that is half covered.
- The tapped stop stays marked as the active row in the list, so tapping through a line does not lose your place.
- Added a Reverse button per line. Nothing in the data says which direction is outbound, so the rider chooses; distances are recomputed from the other end rather than left reading backwards.
